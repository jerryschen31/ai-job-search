# AI Engineering x Biotech Jobs, Bay Area & US-Remote (as of 2026-10-04)

> Generated by the `/job-scraper` workflow on 2026-10-04. 21 scored roles (9 high, 12 medium) plus 12 screened-out roles. Quick fit signals from titles and posting text, not full evaluations.

## Scope and method

- **Definition:** engineering roles that build, deploy or operate AI systems (agents, LLM apps, ML platforms, AI infrastructure) at biotech, pharma, genomics or techbio employers.
- **Locations:** SF Bay Area (strong preference South Bay / Peninsula), or remote within the US.
- **Sources searched (2026-10-04):** Workday (Roche/Genentech, Gilead, BMS, AstraZeneca, Merck, Pfizer, Sanofi, Illumina), Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever biotech/AI company boards, LinkedIn (Bay Area, remote; last 30 days) and Built In. Details were fetched from each posting's own page.
- **Coverage caveats:** Workday returned no South San Francisco postings for Roche/Genentech, so Genentech roles come from LinkedIn only. 10x Genomics' Greenhouse board returned "not found". The Greenhouse and Ashby keyword search is local substring filtering over a curated company list, so unlisted employers are not covered. Built In (SF, South SF, San Jose, remote) surfaced almost no biotech employers; the only life-science-adjacent hit was digital-health (Curai Health, Senior Software Engineer, Agentic AI, remote USA), not scored. `job_search_tracker.csv` does not exist in this repo, so only `seen_jobs.json` was used for deduplication.
- **Not verified:** open/closed state was taken from the live postings at search time; postings can close at any time. Compensation figures are quoted from the postings where given.
- **Filters:** algorithm-heavy and research-scientist roles are listed under "Screened out" rather than scored, per your hard-no.
