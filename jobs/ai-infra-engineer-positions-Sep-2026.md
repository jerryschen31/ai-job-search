## Scope and method

- **Definition:** AI-related **Infrastructure Engineer** roles: the title names infrastructure/infra together with an AI/ML/LLM/GPU/HPC/inference/training/agent term, plus infrastructure roles at AI-native companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Cursor, Scale AI, Databricks, Perplexity, Fireworks, Together).
- **Locations:** San Francisco Bay Area (SF, Peninsula, South Bay, East Bay), or remote within the US. Roles anchored only outside the Bay Area were dropped.
- **Sources searched (live, 2026-09-30):** Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever company boards (curated lists of biotech, AI and tech employers), Workday employer sites, LinkedIn (Bay Area, last 30 days) and Built In (San Francisco, San Jose). Descriptions and requirements come from each posting's own detail page.
- **Excerpts are auto-extracted** from the posting text (role summary and requirements section, or qualification-style sentences when no section was labelled). They are trimmed and may include or miss a line; open the link for the authoritative text.
- **Not exhaustive:** only the portals and company boards above were searched. Some boards failed or timed out during the run (10x Genomics, Fireworks AI [Ashby], Palantir [Lever] on the final pass), so those employers may be under-represented. Postings can close at any time.
- **Excluded on purpose:** engineering-manager and director titles, non-software roles (electrical, mechanical, data-center hardware), security-only platform roles, and platform/infrastructure roles whose title shows no AI link (for example billing, GRC, IAM or trading platforms).
- **Deduplication:** one row per company+title; the employer's own ATS link is preferred, and LinkedIn/Built In copies are listed alongside. Near-identical mass postings are consolidated into one entry.
