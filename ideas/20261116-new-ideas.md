## 1. `ga4gh-mcp` — MCP server over GA4GH DRS / WES / TES / htsget
**Pitch:** Let an agent resolve DRS IDs, submit and monitor WES runs, and request htsget slices, all through one standards-based MCP server with the caller's token passed through.

**Problem & who needs it:** Biobanks, consortia and pharma genomics platforms increasingly expose GA4GH APIs. Agents currently get one-off wrappers around vendor APIs, or nothing. Platform and bioinformatics teams need a single, auditable tool surface instead of N bespoke integrations.

**Why now:**
- GA4GH Cloud WS lists WES-TES-DRS integration with passport delegation as a 2026 goal ([roadmap thread](https://groups.google.com/a/ga4gh.org/g/ga4gh-cloud/c/vGvk0b74UBc)), and DRS 1.5 added cloud metadata and storage status.
- MCP 2026-07-28 is shipping in clients ([blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/)).

**Prior art & gap:**
- Server-side implementations exist: [elixir-cloud-aai/drs-filer](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/drs-filer) (6★), [elixir-cloud-aai/cwl-WES](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/cwl-WES) (18★), [NGS360/GA4GH-WES-API-Service](https://github.com/NGS360/GA4GH-WES-API-Service) (1★, WES on HealthOmics) and [umccr/htsget-rs](https://github.com/umccr/htsget-rs) (59★).
- A search for "ga4gh mcp" returned nothing. My searches did not find a GA4GH-focused MCP server.
- Gap: nobody has built the agent-facing client side with a read-only default, a token passthrough policy and a DRS-compliance-suite style conformance test ([ga4gh/drs-compliance-suite](https://github.com/ga4gh/drs-compliance-suite) exists).

**Why Jerry:** He built cloud genomics data solutions and workflow tooling at Encoded Therapeutics and Genentech, and he builds MCP servers and skills with Claude Code.

**MVP (2–4 weeks):**
- Python FastMCP server with tools `drs_resolve`, `wes_submit`, `wes_status`, `tes_list` and `htsget_ticket`.
- Read-only default, explicit allow-list for submission, per-call audit log.
- Test against `drs-filer` and `cwl-WES` in docker-compose, plus the DRS compliance suite as a CI job.
- Stack: Python, httpx, FastMCP, pytest, Docker.

**Career angle:** Shows standards-based agent integration for regulated genomic data, directly relevant to pharma data platforms.

## 2. `fhir-genreport` — Agent-assisted variant report → validated HL7 Genomics Reporting FHIR bundle
**Pitch:** Take a structured variant call or report and produce a FHIR Genomics Reporting IG bundle, validated with the official FHIR validator, with every field traceable to its source.

**Problem & who needs it:** Clinical genomics labs and translational teams must hand genomic findings to EHRs and trial systems in FHIR. Hand-mapping to the Genomics Reporting IG is tedious and error-prone.

**Why now:**
- The [HL7 Genomics Reporting IG](https://github.com/HL7/genomics-reporting) is actively developed (pushed 2026-11-13).
- FHIR MCP servers are arriving ([wso2/fhir-mcp-server](https://github.com/wso2/fhir-mcp-server), 139★), but they are generic FHIR access layers.

**Prior art & gap:**
- A search for "vcf to fhir" found only [usama-zameer84/vcf-to-fhir-genomics-etl](https://github.com/usama-zameer84/vcf-to-fhir-genomics-etl) (0★, a toy ETL).
- The generic FHIR MCP servers do not know genomics profiles.
- Gap: a profile-aware mapper with validation-in-the-loop and provenance.

**Why Jerry:** He has deep NGS/variant-calling domain knowledge (Mutect2, GATK, VarScan), GxP validation experience and cloud pipeline experience.

**MVP (2–4 weeks):**
- Python library plus CLI mapping a VCF/JSON variant summary to Variant, DiagnosticImplication and Genomic Study profiles.
- Runs the HL7 FHIR validator in Docker and feeds errors back to an LLM-assisted fix loop with a deterministic mapper as the baseline.
- Optional MCP wrapper.
- Synthetic data only.
- Stack: Python, fhir.resources, HAPI validator, pytest.

**Career angle:** Bridges bioinformatics, interoperability standards and agentic AI, which suits clinical-data and translational-informatics roles.

## 3. `gxp-aibom` — AI inventory & CycloneDX AI-BOM for GxP computerized-system records
**Pitch:** Scan a repo and produce an AI-BOM (models, prompts, skills, MCP servers, SDK versions) that also emits the fields a GxP system inventory needs, such as intended use, risk class and validation status.

**Problem & who needs it:** Quality and IT teams in pharma need an inventory of AI-enabled systems and their components. Security AI-BOMs capture provenance but not intended use, human-oversight level or validation state.

**Why now:**
- FDA/EMA principles stress context of use and life-cycle management ([NSF](https://www.nsf.org/gb/en/life-science-regulatory-news/fda-and-ema-release-guiding-principles-of-good-ai-practice-in-drug-development)).
- Annex 22 is expected to land around end of 2026 ([eupry](https://eupry.com/ai/annex-22/)).
- CycloneDX 1.7.2 is current ([spec](https://github.com/CycloneDX/specification)).

**Prior art & gap:**
- [cisco-ai-defense/aibom](https://github.com/cisco-ai-defense/aibom) (119★), [GenAI-Security-Project/aibom-generator](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/aibom-generator) (112★), [sbom-tools](https://github.com/sbom-tool/sbom-tools) (268★), [Lab700xOrg/aisbom](https://github.com/Lab700xOrg/aisbom) (84★) and [k8s-aibom](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/k8s-aibom) (75★).
- All are security- or Kubernetes-oriented.
- The only GxP hits were checklists or toys, such as [gauravpandey36/gxp-ai-validator](https://github.com/gauravpandey36/gxp-ai-validator) (0★).
- Gap: a GxP overlay profile on CycloneDX, as a `properties` namespace plus a validator, not a competing scanner. It should consume the existing tools' output rather than replace them.

**Why Jerry:** He was project lead for a GxP-qualified Bioinformatics Cloud and led FCS Express validation. He also builds the AI tooling being inventoried.

**MVP (2–4 weeks):**
- Define a `gxp:` property namespace and JSON-Schema.
- CLI that merges cisco `aibom` output with a `gxp-system.yaml` (intended use, GAMP category, oversight, validation refs).
- Fails CI when required fields are missing.
- Renders an inventory table as Markdown/HTML.
- Stack: Python, CycloneDX Python library, jsonschema, GitHub Actions.

**Career angle:** Makes him a visible practitioner of AI governance in regulated pharma, a niche with few open-source voices.

## 4. `drs-on-s3` — Terraform module for serverless GA4GH DRS + htsget on AWS
**Pitch:** `terraform apply` gives you a DRS and htsget endpoint over existing S3 genomics data, with Lambda, API Gateway, IAM-scoped presigned URLs and CloudTrail data events.

**Problem & who needs it:** Teams that want to share BAM/CRAM/VCF with partners via GA4GH standards face server deployments (Flask/Gunicorn) or building from scratch. A least-privilege serverless pattern is missing.

**Why now:**
- `htsget-lambda-v0.8.2` was released 2026-08-03 ([htsget-rs](https://github.com/umccr/htsget-rs)).
- The GA4GH federated data push ([Cloud WS roadmap](https://groups.google.com/a/ga4gh.org/g/ga4gh-cloud/c/vGvk0b74UBc)).

**Prior art & gap:**
- [htsget-rs](https://github.com/umccr/htsget-rs) (59★) has a Lambda build but no complete reference infrastructure that I found.
- [drs-filer](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/drs-filer) is Flask-based.
- Gap: the infrastructure-as-code, a DynamoDB-backed DRS object index and a security baseline. I have not verified whether umccr publishes its own CDK stacks.

**Why Jerry:** Terraform, AWS (S3, IAM, Batch), and cloud genomics platform builds are his core strengths.

**MVP (2–4 weeks):**
- Terraform module deploying the htsget-lambda artifact and a small DRS Lambda (Python) over a DynamoDB index.
- Presigned URL expiry, bucket policy and CloudTrail data-event defaults.
- Run `drs-compliance-suite` in CI against a test deployment.
- Stack: Terraform, Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3, Python.

**Career angle:** A concrete public AWS/Terraform artifact for genomics data sharing.

## 5. `nb2pipe` — Agent that promotes an analysis notebook into an nf-core-style Nextflow pipeline, gated by nf-test
**Pitch:** Point Claude Code at a Jupyter/R notebook and get a modular Nextflow pipeline with containers, a schema and nf-test snapshots. The agent can't declare success until the pipeline reproduces the notebook's outputs.

**Problem & who needs it:** Scientists prototype in notebooks, and bioinformatics engineers rewrite them by hand to production. This is a common bottleneck in biotech.

**Why now:**
- Agentic coding is mainstream ([anthropics/claude-code](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code), ~156k★ at the time of query).
- nf-test ([askimed/nf-test](https://github.com/askimed/nf-test), 194★) provides a machine-checkable acceptance criterion, which makes agent loops reliable.

**Prior art & gap:**
- A search for "nextflow agent llm" returned nothing, and "notebook to nextflow" returned nothing.
- [nf-core/modules](https://github.com/nf-core/modules) (438★) supplies reusable modules to reuse instead of generating new ones.
- Gap: the verification-gated conversion workflow, i.e. a notebook output "golden" fixture compared against pipeline outputs.

**Why Jerry:** He has bioinformatics pipeline experience at scale (7,000+ samples), an AI Champion role training colleagues, and Claude Code skills experience.

**MVP (2–4 weeks):**
- A Claude Code skill plus a Python harness.
- Step 1: execute the notebook and capture golden outputs.
- Step 2: the agent selects nf-core modules and writes the workflow.
- Step 3: `nf-test` equivalence check on tolerance-aware comparisons.
- Demo on one public RNA-seq tutorial notebook.
- Stack: Nextflow, nf-test, Python, Claude Code skills, Docker.

**Career angle:** A public demonstration of agentic engineering with verifiable guardrails, for biotech AI engineering roles. It requires no novel algorithm.

## 6. `agent-periodic-review` — Periodic-review reports from agent audit trails (Annex 11 / Part 11 style)
**Pitch:** Ingest agent audit logs (OpenTelemetry-style traces or JSONL) and produce a human-reviewable periodic review package: tool-use summary, policy exceptions, anomalies, model/prompt version changes, and sign-off checklist.

**Problem & who needs it:** Regulated organisations must periodically review audit trails of computerized systems. AI agents generate high-volume traces that no reviewer can read raw, so QA needs a structured summary that points to the evidence.

**Why now:**
- FDA/EMA principles call for life-cycle management and monitoring ([Stephenson Harwood](https://www.stephensonharwood.com/post/102mqoo/fda-and-ema-jointly-issue-guiding-principles-of-good-ai-practice-in-drug-developm/)).
- The draft Annex 22 is expected to be finalised soon ([eupry](https://eupry.com/ai/annex-22/)). It reportedly restricts generative AI from GMP-critical uses, so *non-critical* use needs monitoring evidence.

**Prior art & gap:**
- Searches for "audit trail review" returned only toys: [GxPAuditTrailReview/GxPAuditTrail-AuditTrailReview-0.8](https://github.com/GxPAuditTrailReview/GxPAuditTrail-AuditTrailReview-0.8) (0★) and [imgesu/audit_trail_reviewer](https://github.com/imgesu/audit_trail_reviewer) (1★).
- Log producers (the earlier `agent-ledger` idea) are a different layer. This tool is the consumer and reviewer.
- Gap: deterministic rule-based review with LLM-written narrative only on top of cited evidence.

**Why Jerry:** He has GxP validation experience, and as department AI Champion he already has real agent usage patterns to model.

**MVP (2–4 weeks):**
- Python CLI reading JSONL traces, with a rule pack (e.g. out-of-hours use, new tools, denied calls, version drift).
- Outputs a signed-off-ready Markdown/PDF review report with evidence links, and a synthetic trace generator for tests.
- Stack: Python, DuckDB, Jinja2, pytest.

**Career angle:** Shows he understands the operate-and-monitor half of AI validation, which is the part most AI-in-pharma efforts under-serve.
